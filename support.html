Frequently Asked Questions

How do I scan a document?

Open the app and tap the scan button. Position your device's camera over the document. The app will automatically detect the document edges and capture the scan, then you can review pages before creating the PDF.

Where are my scanned documents stored?

All scanned documents and generated PDFs are stored locally on your device. The app does not upload your files to any server or cloud service.

Can I scan multiple pages into one PDF?

Yes. You can scan multiple pages consecutively and the app will combine them into a single PDF document.

Does the app collect my personal data?

Your scanned files stay on your device unless you explicitly share them. The app does not require accounts, does not upload your PDFs to Cosmic Production servers, and stores only local information needed for document history, daily scan limits, purchase status and preferences.

What does Premium unlock?

The free version includes a daily scan limit. Premium unlocks unlimited scanning with a one-time purchase, so you can keep processing larger document batches on busy days.

Why does the app need camera access?

Camera access is required solely to scan documents. The camera is used only when you actively initiate a scan. No images are sent anywhere outside your device.

How can I share my PDFs?

After creating a PDF, use the share button within the app to send it via email, messaging apps, AirDrop, or any other sharing method available on your device.

The app is not working correctly. What should I do?

Try closing and reopening the app. If the issue persists, ensure you are running the latest version of the app from the App Store. If you continue to experience problems, please contact us at ivan@cosmicproduction.hr with a description of the issue.
