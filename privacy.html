Privacy Policy

Stvori PDF — Last updated: April 10, 2026

Introduction

SoftSplit d.o.o., located in Split, Croatia, develops and publishes Stvori PDF. We are committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how the app handles your information.

Data We Collect

Stvori PDF does not require user accounts and does not upload your scanned documents or generated PDF files to our servers. Core document processing happens locally on your device. The app may, however, use limited device identifiers and ad-request data through Google AdMob in the free version in order to display advertisements.

Camera Usage

The app uses your device's camera solely for the purpose of scanning documents. Camera access is requested only when you initiate a scan. Images captured by the camera are processed locally on your device and are never uploaded or shared with any third party.

Document and PDF Storage

All scanned documents and generated PDF files are stored locally on your device only. SoftSplit d.o.o. has no access to your documents or files. You have full control over your data and can delete it at any time through the app or your device's file system.

Third-Party Services

Stvori PDF uses Google AdMob to display ads in the free version and Apple StoreKit to process in-app purchases. Where required, the app uses Google's User Messaging Platform to present advertising privacy choices. If you grant tracking permission, AdMob may use your device identifier to improve ad relevance and measure advertising performance. We do not use third-party analytics services for product analytics, and we do not sell your personal data.

Children's Privacy

Stvori PDF is not directed to children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children, and document files remain on-device unless you explicitly share them using system sharing features.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected on this page with an updated revision date.
